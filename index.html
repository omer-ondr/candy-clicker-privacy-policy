Candy Clicker Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11.11.2026

This Privacy Policy outlines the data practices of the Candy Clicker mobile application (“Application”).

1. Information We Collect and Process

The Application does not require account registration or login, and does not directly request or collect real names, email addresses, phone numbers, precise location, photos, contacts, or payment information.

Core game progress, language preferences, audio, and visual settings are stored locally on your device. However, to provide online features such as the global leaderboard and application update checks, the Application transmits limited data to our backend database powered by Supabase:

  • Leaderboard & Profile Data: A randomly generated, anonymous player identifier (player_id) created locally on your device, along with your chosen display nickname, selected in-game avatar ID, lifetime candy score, and prestige count. Please do not include real names or sensitive personal information in your nickname, as leaderboard entries are publicly visible to other players.
  • Moderation Reports: If you report an inappropriate nickname on the leaderboard, the randomly generated player IDs of both the reporting player and the reported entry are transmitted to prevent abuse and enforce community standards.
  • App Update Checks: Basic platform information (such as operating system type) is used to query our server for available or required application updates.

2. Advertisements and Third-Party Services

Cloud Infrastructure (Supabase): We use Supabase as our backend service provider to host the global leaderboard, process nickname moderation reports, and serve application update configurations. When the Application communicates with Supabase, standard network metadata (such as your IP address) is processed automatically as part of encrypted HTTPS transmissions for routing and security purposes. You can review the Supabase Privacy Policy for details on their infrastructure security and data handling.

Advertising (Google AdMob): The Application utilizes the Google Mobile Ads SDK / Google AdMob to display banner ads, interstitial ads (e.g., post-prestige), and optional rewarded ads that players can choose to watch to double their offline earnings. Google and its advertising technology partners may automatically collect or share the following data categories for ad serving, measurement, analytics, and fraud prevention:

  • IP address and approximate location derived from it,
  • Android advertising ID and other device/account identifiers,
  • Product interactions, such as app launches, ad views, and ad clicks,
  • Diagnostic data regarding application and SDK performance.

For more information on how the ad provider uses data, you can review the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services. Data transfers to Google and Supabase are encrypted in transit via HTTPS.

3. Personalized Ads and Consent

Advertisements may be personalized or non-personalized. Where required in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, consent will be requested for ad personalization and the processing of related device identifiers. Options to withdraw consent or change privacy preferences are provided via the in-app privacy settings link where mandated by applicable law.

4. Purposes of Data Processing

Data may be processed for the following purposes:

  • storing game progress and preferences locally on the device,
  • operating the public global leaderboard, displaying player rankings, and moderating reported nicknames,
  • checking for and notifying players of application updates,
  • serving ads, measuring ad performance, and preventing ad fraud,
  • detecting bugs, performance issues, and security vulnerabilities within the app and third-party SDKs,
  • complying with legal obligations and protecting rights.

5. Data Retention and Sharing

Locally stored game data remains on your device until you uninstall the app or clear app storage via device settings (which also resets your locally stored random player ID). Leaderboard records (anonymous player ID, nickname, avatar ID, lifetime candy, and prestige count) are retained in our database to maintain the global rankings and are publicly visible to other players within the Application. You may request the deletion of your leaderboard entry at any time by contacting us at the email address below.

The retention and processing of ad-related data are governed by Google’s own privacy policies. Data may be shared with Google, Supabase, and authorized service providers solely to deliver app functionality, ad services, meet regulatory requirements, or maintain security. These third parties are governed by their respective privacy policies.

6. Children’s Privacy

The Application is not directed toward children under 13 years of age, and the developer does not knowingly collect personal information from this age group. If you believe that a child under 13 has provided personal data, please contact us using the details below.

7. Your Rights and Requests

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights regarding access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, objection, and data portability. For inquiries under the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK), or applicable privacy frameworks across the EU/EEA, UK, and Switzerland, reach out via the contact information below. For advertising data processed by Google, you may also utilize Google's dedicated privacy tools.

8. Security

We employ reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to protect data. However, no electronic transmission over the internet or storage method can be guaranteed 100% secure.

9. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated periodically. The updated version will be posted here along with a revised “Last updated” date. In the event of material changes, additional notice will be provided within the Application as appropriate.
